# Code of conduct

Provodnik is a small fan project. Keep it friendly:

- Be respectful. No insults, harassment, slurs or personal attacks.
- Stay on topic: Provodnik and installing G.A.M.M.A.
- No piracy, cheats or hacks, and no links to them.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good faith, especially with newcomers.

Comments, issues and discussions that break these rules may be edited, hidden or deleted, and repeat offenders blocked
from the repository. To report something privately, use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
option on the comment or the profile.
